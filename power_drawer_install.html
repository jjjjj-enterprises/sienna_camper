A

Stop before you trim

this reverses the build sheet

Correction The slides go on the 20" walls, not the 22" ones

I had this backwards on the build sheet, and the trim instruction that came out of it was wrong. The drawer travels in +X, out the side door. A side-mount slide runs along the travel direction, so it lands on the drawer faces whose flat side points fore-and-aft — the walls that span X. Those are the 20" walls, the ones the cut list calls front/back.

The model agrees to the thousandth, which is what settles it:

clear span between the legs = 26.0 − 3.0 = 23" drawer depth = 22" → ½" each side, exactly slide clearance drawer X width = slide length = drawer travel = 20" → a 20" slide on a 20" wall

A 20" slide cannot sit on an 18½" wall, and the ½" gaps at the leg line exist for exactly one reason. So:

The 20" walls stay 20" — full width, they carry the slides. The 22" walls trim to 20½" and fit between them. If you already trimmed the 20" pair to 18½", those two pieces are now short and need recutting; the 22" pair is untouched and still good.

Blocker There is nothing to screw the slides to

The slide's cabinet member has to land on a continuous, plumb, fore-aft face at y = 3" and y = 26", running the full 20" of travel in X. Look at what is actually there at those lines:

• The legs are in the right plane — their inner faces are y = 3" and y = 26" — but a leg is only 1½" wide in X. That is 1½" of bearing under a 20" slide carrying ~39 lb.
• The centre divider and the side rail run fore-aft, square across the travel direction. They are the drawer's end stops, not slide mounts. The plan's Component 2 caption says to mount the slides on them — that instruction cannot be followed, and it is the one line in the build manual that does not survive contact with the geometry.
• The side rail is also only 1½" tall and lives at the very top of the bay (z 16¼–17¾), 2" outboard of the legs.

You need to add two slide-mounting boards before the drawer can hang. They are not in the cut list, the BOM, or any render. Sizes are in section D.

The two boards you have to add

not in the cut list or the BOM

One at each leg line, giving the slide a continuous face exactly in the leg's plane. Screwed to the leg, the divider and the side rail, so the load goes into the frame and not into the board's own fasteners.

PieceQtySizeWhere & why
Slide-mounting board220¾ × 3 × ¾"Spans the bay in X, from the divider face to the side rail's inner face. Its inner face must land flush with the leg's inner face — y = 3" at the front, y = 26" at the back. Set it with its top at 16¼" so the slide and the box top share one datum. ¾" birch off the sheet.
Packer behind each board220¾ × 3 × ¾"Only if you fix the board to the leg's outer face (y = 1½ / 27½) instead of notching it in. Two ¾" layers = the leg's 1½" width, bringing the face back to exactly 3". Same stock, same cut — rip a 6" strip and crosscut four.

Why flush with the leg matters: the drawer's 22" depth was derived from the 23" clear span between the legs. Land the board's face anywhere else and you lose that ½" a side — ¾" proud and the drawer will not go in at all.

E

Install order

frame first, drawer last
Before anything

Recut the two 20" walls if you already trimmed them

They carry the slides and must be the full 20". The 22" pair trims to 20½" instead. Section A has the arithmetic.

Make and fit the two mounting boards

Dry-fit both, then check the gap between their faces reads 23" at the front of the bay, the middle and the back. This is the one measurement that decides whether the drawer goes in — get it before any glue.

Screw the boards home

Into the leg first, then the divider and the side rail. Check plumb on each board with a short level before the last screws — a board leaning in at the top pinches the drawer at the top of its travel and nowhere else, which is a maddening fault to chase later.

Mount the cabinet members

Top edge of each slide at 16¼", front end flush with the outboard edge of the bay so the drawer pulls clear. Use a spacer block cut to the height rather than measuring each screw — two slides that differ by 1/16" will bind.

Mount the drawer members on the box

On the outer faces of the 20" walls, at whatever offset down from the box top your slide's instructions give. Same spacer block, same reference edge, both sides.

Hang it empty and run it

Full travel, twenty times. It should coast the last few inches shut under its own weight. If it drags at one end, the boards are not parallel; if it drags at the top of travel, one is out of plumb.

Load-test before you trust it

50 lb of sandbag, full extension, leave it out for an hour. ~78 lb on a 100 lb pair is a ~22% margin — thin enough to be worth proving before two EcoFlows are riding on it.

Fit the catch

Friction catch or turn latch at the outboard wall. Not optional: 48 lb rolling itself open on a bend is what takes the slides with it.

Fixtures, then the units

Four cleats bracketing the 15.95 × 15.7" footprint, two D-rings on the 22" walls (the strap runs across the 20" width), and the 1" grommet in a 20" wall — not a 22" one, those faces now carry the slides.

DELTA 3 Plus outboard at the pull wall, Smart Extra Battery inboard, both down over the cleats, strap cinched.

Connect and dress the cable

Fridge DC to the Plus's 12V outlet via the fused car-plug→SAE adapter (P1); AC charging cord to the AC input. Pull the drawer to full extension and leave a loop of slack there — a cable dressed with the drawer shut is a cable that unplugs itself the first time you open it.

Geometry read from platform.scad (drawer_module, the leg placement in module_frame) and params.scad, September 2026. Figures are to scale in both axes.

Two things here contradict the build plan and are not yet fixed in it: Component 2's step-3 caption puts the slides on the centre divider and the outer rail, which the travel direction rules out; and the two slide-mounting boards appear in no cut list, BOM or render. The drawer's own dimensions are right — it is the mounting that was never specified.
