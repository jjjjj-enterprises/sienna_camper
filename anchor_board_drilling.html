A

Check before you drill

1 check · 2 methods

Check Which edge is which

The driver edge is the outer edge of the 2½" strip. The strip tips are the free ends of the three strips, the tailgate end. The crossbar's inside edge is the one facing the strips, not its outer edge.

Pencil DRIVER, TIPS and FRONT on the board before you mark anything, and mark on the top face.

Method Mark the riser T-nuts from the risers, not the drawing

As built, the tray's aprons are ⅝" thick, not ¾", so the tray is 19⅛" across, not 19⅜", and the kitchen riser sits about ¼" closer to the driver side than the drawing shows. Screw the tray to the slides first (Fridge Slides sheet, bench fit), set the whole assembly on the comb with each riser's back end ½" in from its strip tip, and mark the T-nuts through the flange holes.

Expect the driver-strip T-nuts at about ¾" from the driver edge and the middle-strip ones at about 23" (not 23¼"). The tongue T-nuts in the crossbar don't change.

Method Drill from the top, counterbore from below

5/16" straight through from the top with step bit #4, then turn the board over and run the same bit back into each hole until the ¾" step has cut just deep enough for the T-nut's flange, about 1/16". Tap the T-nut in with a hammer and a bolt-sized punch, and test each with a bolt.

Drill the tongues

Mark one end of each tongue BACK. Two holes on the centerline, ¾" and 2½" from the back end (Fig 2). Punch, ⅛" pilot, step bit #1 to 9/32", flip and finish from the other side, deburr. Grind the corners round and ease every edge.

Label the board

Top face up. Pencil DRIVER on the 2½" strip's outer edge, TIPS on the strip ends, FRONT on the crossbar.

Mark the riser T-nuts and check them with the risers

Driver strip: ¾" from the driver edge. Middle strip: 23¼" from the driver edge. Both at 2½", 9", 16" and 22½" from the strip tips (Fig 1). Then lay each riser on, back end ½" in from the tip, and confirm through its holes.

Mark the tongue T-nuts

In the crossbar: 14¼" and 31¾" from the driver edge, each at ¾" and 2½" in from the crossbar's inside edge. Lay a drilled tongue on to check, its back end flush with the inside edge.

Drill all twelve, then counterbore

Step bit #4 to 5/16" straight through from the top. Turn the board over and counterbore each to ¾", about 1/16" deep (Fig 3).

Seat the T-nuts and test

Pull each T-nut in from below with a bolt and fender washer from above until its flange is flush (Fig 4), prongs along the strip (Fig 5). A dab of epoxy under each flange, none in the threads. Lay a straightedge across the underside: nothing proud. Thread a bolt into every one by hand to check it runs straight.
