A

Check before you cut

3 changes · 1 method · 2 carry-overs

The frame is Panels A and B again with different lengths. What's new is the deck, the wall and the rear corners, and the plan gets three things about the wall wrong.

Changed The front wall sits behind the legs, so it can't screw to the rails

The legs lap against the front rail's inner face, and the wall goes on the legs' back faces, on the fridge side. That puts the wall 1-7/16" behind the rails (Fig 2). The plan's "2 screws into the top rail and 2 into the bottom rail" can't reach: the top rail is above the wall and in front of it, and the bottom rail is in front of the legs.

Fix: glue three 6" 2×2 offcuts behind the bottom rail, between the legs. They're the same thickness as the legs, so they come out flush with the legs' back faces, and the wall screws to the legs and the blocks: 7 screws.

Keep the wall off the floor. At 17" tall on the floor it's ¾" longer than it needs to be, and it would touch the floor before the feet do, so the panel would rock on it. Set its bottom edge ½" below the leg bottoms. With the feet at 1" that's ½" of air under it, and its top lands ¼" under the deck.

Move the grommet to 2¼" from the driver edge. At the plan's 3", the 1" hole runs to 3½", and the leg starts at 3-7/16" on the other face, so the grommet's flange lands on the leg. At 2¼" it clears by more than ½".

Check You bought 3" round vents, and the wall is drawn for a 9 × 2" slot

The plan draws the low intake as a 9 × 2" louver slot under the fan. The shopping list has a 4-pack of 3" round snap-in soffit vents, and the cabinet-door vent it was also meant for has been deleted, so you have four spares.

Cut three 3" holes in a row instead (Fig 3): centres 7⅜", 11⅛" and 14⅞" from the driver edge, 4⅛" up from the wall's bottom edge. That's about 21 sq in open, more than the slot's 18 and the fan's 17.7, with ¾" of ply between holes. Check the hole size on the vent's package first, and that its clips hold in ½" ply. If they don't, glue the vents in.

Method The deck, the cleats and the corner brackets all want the rails' inner faces

The deck's edge covers the top ¾" of every rail's inner face, and the cleat under it covers the next ¾". A 2" corner bracket fitted the way it went on Panels A and B lands on both.

Fit each corner bracket with its top edge ¾" below the rail top, level with the cleat tops, so the deck's corner rests on it. Stop every cleat 2⅛" short of each corner so the bracket fits in the gap. That makes the cleats 36¼" (front, between the legs), 38⅞" (rear) and 28⅝" (each side), all cut from the strips you already ripped.

Carry-over Match the deck to the bed by raising A and B, never by lowering C

Panel C's deck has to be flush with the bed platform on A and B, which is A/B's rail top plus ¾". As built, A's rail top is about 17½", so its platform lands at 18¼", ¼" under Panel C's 18½".

Don't close that by lowering Panel C. The fridge stack is 16.67" tall and slides out under the rear end rail, whose underside is 17" with the feet at 1". Lower Panel C ¼" and there's under ⅛" left. Turn A's and B's feet out ¼" instead. Also, the rear legs are butted under the rail, so with all four feet at 1" the rear rail sits 1/16" lower than the front. Turn the rear feet out 1/16" more than the front.

Carry-over The control cluster no longer fits where it's drawn

The electrics plan puts the control cluster on the wall's Panel B face at 40.4–45.4" from the driver edge, "over the passenger front leg". The wall now sits behind that leg, so the leg stands 1-7/16" proud of the wall right in the middle of the cluster's footprint. None of this changes what you cut today. Just don't put anything in that column. The cluster gets re-sited when the electrics go in (Component 6).

Build order

same bench method as A and B

Cut the end rails, then measure for the side rails

Two at 46". Stand them 35¾" apart outside to outside and cut the side rails to the gap, about 32⅞".

Glue up the ring upside down, in four corner clamps

Rail tops on the garage floor, as on A and B. Check the diagonals while the glue is wet: both should read 58¼".

Two screws a corner, then the corner brackets, ¾" off the floor

2 × 2½" SPAX through the end rail's face into the side rail's end. Then set each bracket on a ¾" spacer (a cleat offcut) so its edge sits ¾" below the rail top, level with where the cleats will go (Flag 3).

Lap the two 17½" front legs

2" block against the side rail, leg against the block, lapped to the front rail's inner face with its top flush with the rail top. Clamp, pilot, 2 × 2½" through the rail face into the leg.

Front bottom rail and the three back blocks

Still upside down: the 46" rail across the front legs' outer faces, underside flush with the leg bottoms, glued. 2 screws per leg, ⅜" either side of centre and about ¾" up, to miss the foot bore. Then glue the three 6" blocks behind it, between the legs, centred about 14", 23" and 32" from the driver end and flush with the legs' back faces. Drive 2 screws through the rail's outer face into each block, about 1" in from each end of the block.

Turn it over onto the rear legs

Stand the frame up on its front legs, and stand the two 16" legs under the rear corners, flush with both outside faces. Glue the leg tops, then pilot and countersink 2 × 2½" down through the rear rail's top into each leg. Then the 2" L-bracket on each leg's forward face, flat leg under the side rail (Fig 4).

Cut the wall's openings, then hang it

On the bench: the 4¾" fan hole, the three 3" vent holes and the 1" grommet hole at 2¼" (Fig 3). Then stand the wall against the front legs' back faces, on the fridge side, with its bottom edge ½" below the leg bottoms. With the feet at 1" that's two ½" ply offcuts under it. Clamp, pilot, and drive 7 × 1¼" from the fridge side: 2 into each leg and 1 into each block.

The cleats

Trim them to fit (Flag 3) and glue and screw them to the rails' inner faces every 6", tops ¾" below the rail top. Use a deck offcut as the gauge. The front cleat goes between the legs only.

Fit, notch and fix the deck

Measure the opening and trim the deck 1/16" under it. Drop it in, mark the two front legs off the real thing, lift it out and cut the 1½" notches (Fig 5). Drop it back in. It should be flush with the rail tops all round. Screw it into the cleats every 8", countersunk flush, because the mattress lies on it.

Set the height against A and B

In the van, or on a flat floor beside them: Panel C's deck at 18½", the rear feet 1/16" further out than the front. Bring A and B up to meet it, not C down to meet them (Flag 4). Check that the fridge still slides under the rear rail.
