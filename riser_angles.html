A

Check before you cut

1 method · 2 checks · 1 carry-over

Angle steel is an L: two legs, each 2" wide and 3/16" thick. On a riser one leg stands upright and the other, the flange, lies flat on the board. Only one flange gets cut.

Method The flanges point away from the tray

The tray's aprons hang ⅜" off the floor, below the board's top, right beside each riser's upright. A flange pointing toward the tray would sit where the apron runs.

So both flanges point outboard: the driver one toward the van's side wall, the kitchen one toward the kitchen unit.

Check The driver riser goes in as bought

Its full 2" flange reaches ¼" past the board's driver edge, about 1" off the floor. Nothing is there: the rear leg is behind where the riser starts, and the van's side wall is farther out. All four bolts still land in the strip.

upright back at 1¾" from the driver edge − 2" flange = ¼" past the edge

Check The kitchen riser's flange is cut to 1"

A full 2" flange would reach 24⅝" from the driver edge and run under the kitchen unit, whose edge sits at about 24". The unit can't move; it already stands against Panel C's rear leg.

upright back at 22⅝" + 1" flange = 23⅝" · middle strip ends at 24" · ⅜" inside it

Carry-over Drill with the anchor board

The flange bolt holes and the slide's screw holes are laid out on the anchor board sheet, with the T-nuts. Don't drill the angles before then.

Build order

grinder · cut-off wheel
Done

Cut both angles to 24"

Square cuts, burrs ground off. Mark one DRIVER and the other KITCHEN.

Done

Mark the kitchen line

Upright leg up, flange flat. Measure 1" across the flange from the back of the upright at both ends and the middle, and join the marks with a straightedge.

Done

Rip the kitchen flange

Clamped flat with the waste hanging past the bench edge (Fig 6). A shallow scoring pass the full length, then light passes 6–8" at a time, moving the clamps along. Cut dry, wheel square, face shield and gloves, sawdust swept away first.

Done

Clean up and check

Grind the cut edge straight and smooth, round the corners at both ends, and check 1" along the full 24".

Later

Drill with the anchor board

Four ¼-20 clearance holes per flange and the slide's screw holes in each upright, laid out on the anchor board sheet.
