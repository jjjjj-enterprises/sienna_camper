Notes. The fridge and kitchen unit are bought products — no plywood/lumber for them. The rear pantry is bought (prefab drawer units on Panel C's deck) — no lumber at all. Legs come in three batches now (they are no longer all 16"): Panels A/B are 16.75" and Panel C's front pair 17.5", both lapped to the end rails with their tops flush; only Panel C's 2 rear corner legs are 16" and butt-under. Each is cut 1" short and the leveling foot makes up the difference. Bottom rails sit at the leg bottoms (underside 1").

Joinery: plywood face joints get Ryobi R-series biscuits — R1 on the kitchen drawer (1/2"), R3 on the battery drawer (its walls went to 3/4" in Aug 2026, R1 would be lost in that thickness). 2×2 frame corners get 2× 2-1/2" screws + glue + a steel corner bracket — upsized from 2" in Sept 2026, because the screw crosses a full 1.5" rail before it bites and a 2" screw leaves only 1/2" of engagement. Full method in the plan's Joinery & Fastener Guide. The rear pantry is bought (4 IRIS 12"W stackable drawers) and Panel B stores in 2 Sterilite 28-Qt lidded totes beside the spare-tire kit — nothing to cut for either beyond the hold-down cleats.
