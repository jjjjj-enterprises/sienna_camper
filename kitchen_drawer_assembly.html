A

Check before you start

2 checks · 2 methods

Method The front and back cover the sides' ends

The 18" front and back run the box's full width, and the sides fit between them. The front is then one clean face with no end grain showing, and it covers the ½" slide gaps when the drawer is shut.

Size the sides from your ply's real thickness: 26" − 2 × thickness, so 25" for ½" ply and 25-1/16" for 15/32".

Check The slides want exactly ½" per side

The Liberty slides need ½" between the box and each cheek: 18" box, 19" between the cheeks. Too tight and they bind; too loose and they fall out of their tracks.

The safest way to hold 19" is to let the drawer set it: put the slides on the box and the cheeks, run the box into the cheeks, and hang all of it as one unit.

Method The bottom goes under the walls

The 18 × 26" bottom is the box's full outside size, so it glues and screws on underneath the four walls; there's no rabbet. #8 × 1¼" screws up through it into the walls' edges, 3/32" pilots centered on the ½" edge.

Method Hold it up on the kitchen unit

To hang the cheeks, set the kitchen unit in place and lay ¾" of ply on its lid: that holds the drawer and cheeks at 12-5/16" while you drive the screws down through the deck.

Build order

Mark and slot the corners

X on every inside face, TOP on every top edge, TAILGATE on the front. Two R1 slots per corner at 1" and 3" up: in the sides' end grain, and in the front and back's inside faces ¼" in from their ends (Fig 2). Test on scrap first.

Dry-fit

Biscuits in, no glue. Corners tight, tops flush, diagonals equal (~31⅝").

Glue up the walls

Glue in the slots and on the joints, clamp, check the diagonals, wipe off squeeze-out. Let it set.

Bottom on

Glue the walls' bottom edges, set the box on the bottom with the edges flush all round, then #8 × 1¼" screws up through the bottom into each wall about every 6", 1" from the corners. 3/32" pilots centered on the ½" edges.

Slides onto the box and the cheeks

Follow Liberty's sheet (Fig 5). Press the release lever and pull each drawer member off. Mark 1¼" up from the bottom of the box on each side and screw the drawer member on with its top edge on the line and its front at the side's front end, behind the ½" front. Clip a cabinet member onto it, measure X from the box's bottom to the cabinet member's top edge, unclip, and mark X up from each cheek's bottom edge. Screw the cabinet members on with their top edges on that line, set ½" + 1/16" back from the cheeks' tailgate ends. #6 × ½" pan heads, slotted holes first.

Assemble the unit

Run the box into the cheeks. It should glide and soft-close, the box front flush with the cheeks' ends and its bottom level with theirs.

Pilot through the deck

Mark ten screw spots on the deck top (Fig 4): 2-7/16" and 21-15/16" from the passenger edge, at 3", 9", 15", 21" and 27" from the tailgate edge. Drill ⅛" straight through, then countersink from the top.

Hang it

Kitchen unit in place, ¾" ply on its lid. Set the drawer unit on it under the deck: outer cheek tight against the side cleat, both cheeks starting 2-3/16" in from the tailgate edge. Check each pilot hole lands on a cheek's top edge, then drive the #8 × 2" screws down through the deck. Then 3 screws through the outer cheek into the cleat.

Catch and test

Magnetic catch at the back. Pull out to full extension and back: clears the kitchen unit's lid, passes under the rear rail, closes soft.
