Switch Plate

The FRIDGE and FANS switches in a ½" plywood plate, held 1½" off Panel C's front wall by one 2x2 block so their bodies and wires have room behind. It is the top of the control cluster in the driver column, above the W1209 (Fridge Bay Cooling sheet). The switches are glued in with E6000. The fridge socket is not on this plate (see below).

Build

bench

Cut the two parts

Plate: 4½ × 2" from a ½" ply offcut. Block: a 2" crosscut off a 2x2 offcut (1½ × 1½ × 2"). Square ends on the block matter: it's what holds the plate flat to the wall.

Lay out

On the plate's good face: hole centers ¾" in from each end, 1" down. Screw centers on the middle line (2¼" from either end), ½" from the top and bottom. Pencil the block's outline on the back, 1½" in from each end.

Drill the switch holes

The Ampper sheet gives a 20mm mounting hole: a 13/16" spade or Forstner bit. Drill a test hole in scrap first and check the switch slides in snug. Drill the plate from the front face with scrap clamped behind so the back doesn't splinter.

Dry-fit the switches

Push each one in from the front. The snap clips on the sides are made for thin panels and will fight ½" ply; if a switch won't seat, snip its clips off flush with side cutters. The glue holds it instead.

Glue on the block

Wood glue, block centered on the back inside its outline, clamped for an hour. Then drill the two screw holes through the plate and block (⅛" pilot, then 5/32" through the plate) and countersink the front so the heads sit flush.

Glue in the switches

Sand the edges. Thin bead of E6000 on the back of each bezel, push the switch in, both turned the same way (ON at the top), and tape across them while it cures: 24 hours before you push spades on. Keep glue out of the rocker and off the tabs.

Wire, then mount

Push the spades on per Fig 5 of the Fridge Bay Cooling sheet: PWR from the fuse, LOAD out, GND to the NEG bus. Then hold the plate at x 1½–6", bottom at z 13½", and drive the two #8 × 2¼" screws. To get at the wires later, back out the two screws and the whole plate comes forward.

Why the fridge socket isn't on the plate

it lives in the fridge bay

Socket Next to the fridge, not the switches

The fridge's cord ends in a car plug about 1" across, and it would have to reach from the fridge's power port at the tailgate end, through the wall, to this plate. The plug won't pass the 1" grommet beside the other wires, and the fridge slides out, so its cord needs slack at the fridge end anyway. The socket (Nilight 10027W: socket on a 3 ft 16AWG lead) lives in the bay instead. Its lead runs through the grommet to the cluster, and the socket is zip-tied to the driver fixed rail by the fridge's power port. Cut its red ring terminal off and crimp a ¼" female spade for the FRIDGE switch's LOAD tab; its black goes to the NEG bus.
