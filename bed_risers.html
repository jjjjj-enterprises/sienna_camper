as-built correction

Changed Why this sheet exists

Panels A and B were built to a deck height and length that turned out wrong in the van: the front seats had been assumed all the way forward, and the floor under A and B is lower than planned. This sheet is the as-built fix; the plan's real fix is longer legs. Built this? Feedback wanted. These changes correct mistakes found while building. If you build from this plan, feedback on the corrections (whether they worked in your van, and what you'd do differently) would be very much appreciated.

Steps

bench, then van

Panel A stacks

Crosscut nine 2x6s at 29". Glue and screw them in threes (#9 × 2½" through the top layer into the one below, 6 per layer, staggered), crowns all the same way. Set one stack on each side rail with its outside edge on the bed's outside edge, and one centered on the divider. Screw up through each 2x2 rail into its stack from inside the frame: #9 × 3", three per stack.

Panel B 1x6s

Crosscut three 1x6s at 23⅞" (the frame's length). Lay one on each side rail, outside edge on the bed's outside edge, and one centered across the bearer. Screw each down into its rail or bearer with #8 × 2" flat heads, four per board.

Leg blocks for Panel B's windshield end

From the 2x6 offcuts, cut four pieces about 5½ × 4⅜" and glue and screw them in pairs (#9 × 2½"). In the top of each, bore a ¼"-deep pocket with a 1½" Forstner bit for the foot's pad, so it can't walk off. Set one under each windshield-end leg, then fine-tune with the feet. Add or drop a layer if the floor wants more or less.

Bed on top, then level

Panel A's bed piece back on, screwed down through its rails into the side stacks with #8 × 2" (4 per rail). Panel B's halves drop on loose. Turn Panel A's feet out ¼", Panel B's tailgate feet in ¼" and its windshield feet to suit, and check the deck with a straightedge across the A/B and B/C seams before the mattress goes on.
