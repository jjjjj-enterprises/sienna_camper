Changed Runners under the legs, not risers on the frames

This sheet was the Bed Risers sheet (1 Nov): 2x6 stacks on Panel A's frame, 1x6s on Panel B's and 2x6 blocks under B's windshield-end legs. In the van the floor padding gave under the feet, so the lift moved to the floor on long boards that spread each leg's load. The frame-top risers and B's leg blocks are dropped. Built this? Feedback wanted. These changes correct mistakes found while building. If you build from this plan, feedback on the corrections (whether they worked in your van, and what you'd do differently) would be very much appreciated.

Checked Height, in the van

Tried in the van on 7 Nov: the 3¾" runners work, with the feet taking up the rest. If yours sit low, add a second 1x6 to each runner (4½"). The ply foot pads don't change the height, because the feet drop through their holes onto the 1x6.

Steps

bench, then van

Take the risers off

If the 1 Nov frame-top boards or B's leg blocks are on, take them off. The bed platform goes straight onto the frame rails: Panel A's piece screwed down through its rails into the frame (#8 × 2", 4 per rail), Panel B's halves loose.

Measure and cut

Cut four 2x6s and two 1x6s at 34". That runs from Panel A's windshield edge to about 2" past Panel B's windshield-end leg. Make sure the runner clears the front seat tracks with the seats all the way back.

Laminate

Glue and screw each runner as one board (Figs 2 and 3), end-grain arcs all curving the same way. Roll glue over the whole face of the bottom 2x6, lay the middle 2x6 on it, and drive the first screws down through it: #9 × 2½", 1¾" in from each edge, every 6" starting 3" from the end. Glue the top of that and lay on the 1x6, then drive the second screws through it, 1" in from each edge, between the first ones. Heads flush. Wipe off the squeeze-out and let the glue set overnight before it takes weight.

Foot pads

Cut six 4¼" squares from the 4¼" × 28" thin-ply offcut (6 × 4¼" plus saw kerfs is about 26¼"; the 3¾" × 13¾" piece is a spare for a seventh). Drill a 1½" hole through the center of each (Forstner bit or hole saw, scrap underneath so it doesn't tear out). Lay the runners in the van, set the panels on them and mark each foot. Center a pad on each mark, glue it and drive two #8 × 2" flat heads in opposite corners. Each foot drops through its hole and stands on the 1x6, so the runner can't slide out from under it and the height doesn't change.

Set and level, loaded

Runners centered on the leg lines (the heat vents are farther back, by Panel C's windshield end; notch a runner if it reaches one). 6" pieces of 1x6 under Panel B's two tailgate-end legs and Panel C's driver-side windshield-end leg. Panel C's tailgate-end legs stand on the old comb's metal straps, laid front to back under the anchor board. Set the feet with the deck loaded so the padding has already compressed, and check with a straightedge across the A/B and B/C seams.
