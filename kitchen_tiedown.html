Kitchen Tie-Down

The kitchen unit sits on the folded seat between the anchor board's middle and passenger strips and is held by two 1" ratchet straps over its top: the front one hooked to seat-latch hoops H2 and H4, the rear one from an eye bolt on the middle strip to a footman loop on the passenger strip. No holes in the van.

Steps

board now · van last

Footman loop

On the passenger strip, lengthwise, 9½–12½" from the hatch (the strip starts 3" from the hatch, so 6½–9½" from its back end), centered across. Mark the holes from the loop, drill 3/32" pilots about ½" deep, and drive two #8 × ⅝" flat heads.

Rear eye bolt

Take out the button head in the kitchen riser's second T-nut. Thread a nut onto a Hillman ¼" eye bolt, hold the button head beside it, cut the shank to the same length, and back the nut off to clean the cut threads. Screw it in with a washer, eye toward the passenger side.

Dry-fit the straps

Set the kitchen unit in place and lay both straps over it in their notches. Check that neither rubs a sharp edge, that each ratchet lands on the passenger half of the top, and that the front hooks reach H2's and H4's bars.

In the van: last

After the fridge tray is bolted down and its chains are tightened with the fridge loaded: kitchen unit in, both straps hooked, ratcheted snug (snug, not cranked), tails tied off. Push the unit hard front, back and sideways: it shouldn't move. Check again after the first drive.
