A

Check before you cut

2 checks · 2 methods · 1 carry-over

Four notches across Panel C's front edge, two across Panel B's back edge. Their positions come from the van and their heights come from the steel, so none of them depends on how the feet end up set.

Method Building fresh: notch before you assemble

On a built frame these are saw-and-chisel cuts with the panel upside down. On loose parts they take a few minutes: cut the rail and block notches before the rail and blocks go on, and the wall's notches with its other openings.

And keep every screw out of the two 3" bands, at 12¾–15¾" and 30¼–33¼" from the driver end: in the rails, the blocks, the wall and the footman loops. The wall's screw into each block goes at least 1¼" up from its bottom edge.

Check The van sets the tongue's height, not the board

Each tongue ends lying flat on its seat-track cap, whose top is 1" off the floor (measured in August). A shim under the tongue's lap on the crossbar brings that end up level with the cap, so the bar runs flat at 1" to 1-3/16" the whole way. A thicker or thinner mat changes the shim, not the bar.

cap top 1" + bar 3/16" = 1-3/16" · notch tops 1½" · 5/16" clear

Those heights are at Panel C's lowest foot setting, and C sits within 1/16" of it. Turning any foot out only adds clearance.

Check The tongue lines are measured

On 13 Oct, with Panel C in place, the two cap holes measured 14¼" and 31¾" from C's driver-side face: centered on the panel and 17½" apart, as the model assumed. Both lines land inside the two outer back blocks, and well clear of the front legs at 3-7/16" to 4⅞" in from each side.

Each notch is 3" wide, 1½" each side of its line. That's the 2" bar plus ½" each side, for the panels landing a little off side to side.

Method The wall hangs lower, so its notch is deeper

Panel C's front wall stops ½" off the floor. The front bottom rail and the back blocks behind it stop at the leg bottoms, 1" off the floor. Panel B's end bottom rail is the same as C's.

So the wall is notched 1" deep and the rail, blocks and B's rail ½" deep. Every notch top then lands at 1½". Cut them with each panel upside down on the bench.

Carry-over The strap notches come later

The three ratchet straps from the crossbar's D-rings to the 3rd-row striker loops cross the same four parts. How high they cross depends on the D-ring fittings, which aren't bought yet.

Once the board is in the van with its D-rings on, tension each strap and mark its notch from the webbing. Expect about 1¼" wide.

Build order

about an hour
Done

Measure the cap holes

13 Oct, with Panel C in place: 14¼" and 31¾" from its driver-side face.

Done

Lay out Panel C

Panel C upside down on the bench. On the front bottom rail's bottom face, mark 12¾", 15¾", 30¼" and 33¼" from the driver-side face. Square each line across the rail, the block behind it and the wall's bottom edge.

Done

Mark the depths

On the rail's outer face and the block's inner face, ½" from their bottoms. On both faces of the wall, 1" from its bottom edge.

Done

Cut the rail and block together

Handsaw or multi-tool down both edge lines to the ½" line, through the rail and the block in one go. Add a few kerfs between, then chisel out to the line. The wall's screw into each block sits about 1¾" off the floor, above the notch.

Done

Cut the wall

Jigsaw or multi-tool, 3" wide by 1" up, at both lines. Ease the corners with sandpaper.

Done

Panel B

Upside down on the bench. On the bottom end rail that meets Panel C, mark the same four lines from B's driver-side face and cut both notches ½" deep.

Later

Check in the van, then the straps

With the board and tongues in: the bar should have about ½" each side and 5/16" over it at all four crossings. Then tension the striker straps and mark their notches from the webbing.
