  1. 1Rubber mat, full floor, as built. No cleats: the mat's grip, plus the strap's down-pull, keeps the units from walking.
  2. 2D-rings ×2, one on the inside face of each side wall, about 5½" up: mark the screw center ~7½" down from the box's top edge. #8 × ⅝" screws, 3/32" pilot. The wall is ¾" and the slide is screwed to the other face, so keep the screws ⅝" or shorter and well above the slide's band.
  3. 3Cam strap, across the 18½" width, over both units. Run it under the tray if you build one. Cinch it before the van moves.
  4. 4Turn latch on the frame's top rail, tongue down over the pull wall. This keeps the drawer shut. It's separate from the strap and not optional: 78 lb rolling open on a bend takes the slides with it. A friction catch also works. Bought: Wright Products V20A 1¾" (screw ~⅜" above the rail's bottom edge so it reaches ~1" onto the wall), or make a 3" × ¾" ply tab on a #8 × 1½" screw.
  5. 5Cord slot, as built, near the back of the left side wall. Leave slack for full extension.
  6. 620" slides, low on the side walls' outer faces. For reference only: they're the reason the D-rings go high.

Drawn 30 Oct 2026 from the bench photos (drawer-05 to drawer-08). The DELTA 3 is on the cord-slot side, as photographed. Which side wall faces the cab isn't recorded, so the views say "side wall" rather than fore or aft.
