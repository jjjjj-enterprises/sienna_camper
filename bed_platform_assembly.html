A

Check before you assemble

2 methods · 2 checks

Every piece is the same idea: two 29" rails lying flat, slats butted between their inner edges, the first and last slat flush with the rail ends.

Method Assemble face down on a flat floor

The pocket holes go on the slats' underside, so the mattress side never sees a screw. Lay every part mattress-side down on a flat floor, drill and screw from above, and the floor makes the top faces flush for you.

Every slat end is glued and screwed: glue on the slat's end, then the two pocket screws, which clamp the joint while the glue cures.

Method Two jigs: the pocket-hole jig and spacer blocks

Pocket-hole jig (Kreg 320): it clamps to a board and guides a stepped bit at 15°, drilling an angled pocket into the slat's underside that a screw runs through into the rail's edge. Set the drill guide and the bit's depth collar for ¾" stock.

Spacers: strips of plywood ripped to the gap width and cut 13" long, laid flat between slats so every gap comes out equal without measuring. The 13" length keeps them from twisting or tipping. Rip two at 2⅞" for Panel A and two at 3⅛" for Panel B.

Check The narrow slat goes in the middle of each B half

One 1×4 went to the fridge tray's aprons, so two of Panel B's ten slats are 2½"-wide 1×3. Put one in the middle of each half. That half's four gaps open to 3⅛"; Panel A's stay at 2⅞".

Panel A: (29 − 5 × 3½) ÷ 4 = 2⅞" · Panel B half: (29 − 4 × 3½ − 2½) ÷ 4 = 3⅛"

Check Square before you screw

Measure both diagonals before the last screws go in: about 56-15/16" on Panel A's piece and 37-15/16" on each B half. Equal diagonals mean it's square; push the long corner in until they match.

G

Build order

about 2 hours

Sort and face the parts

Three piles: Panel A (2 rails, 5 × 42"), and two B halves (2 rails, 4 × 17½" 1×4 and 1 × 17½" 1×3 each). Sight down each slat: if it bows, the crown (the hump) goes up, on the mattress side, so weight flattens it instead of adding to the sag. Otherwise pick the better face. Pencil an X on the other face, the underside.

Drill the pocket holes

Jig and bit collar set for ¾". Two holes in each end of every slat, on the X face: ⅞" in from each edge on the 1×4s, ¾" on the 1×3s. 60 holes in all. Run a test hole in a scrap first.

Rip the spacers

Measure your slats first. A 1×4 is often 3¼–3⅜" wide, not 3½", and five slightly narrow slats leave about 1" extra in the last gap. Work out the gaps from your boards: Panel A = (29 − 5 × slat width) ÷ 4; B half = (29 − 4 × 1×4 width − 1×3 width) ÷ 4. The 2⅞" and 3⅛" below are for full 3½" and 2½" boards. Then from ½" or ¾" ply scrap: rip two strips at 2⅞" and two at 3⅛", then cut all four to 13". Pencil the width on each so they don't get mixed up.

Lay out Panel A's piece face down

On a flat floor, mattress side down: the two rails 49" apart outside to outside, the five 42" slats between them, X faces up. First and last slat flush with the rail ends; 2⅞" spacers in each gap.

Glue and screw it, squaring as you go

Wood glue on each slat end before it goes in. End grain soaks glue up, so brush on a thin coat, wait a minute, then add a second coat. Clamp a rail and the end slat, drive that end's two pocket screws, then the other end slat. Check the diagonals (56-15/16"), then fill in the middle slats against the spacers. A bowed slat touches the floor in the middle with its ends lifted: press each end flat beside the rail while you drive its screws. Snug, not stripped: pine strips easily. Wipe off squeeze-out, and leave it flat for an hour before moving it.

Build the two B halves the same way

Glue and pocket screws at every slat end, the same way. Rails 24½" apart outside, the 1×3 slat in the middle, 3⅛" spacers. Diagonals about 37-15/16".

Ease the edges

Sand the top edges and corners round so nothing wears the mattress cover.

Fit Panel A's piece and screw it down

Mattress side up on Panel A, 1½" over each side, ends flush with Panel A's frame. Pilot and countersink, then #8 × 2": 4 through each rail into the side rail, about 2½" from each end and two between, and 5 through each end slat into the end rail, about 8" apart.

Drop in the B halves

Center bearer in place first. Each half sits with its outer rail on B's side rail and its inner rail on the bearer, against Panel A's piece at one end and Panel C's deck at the other. No fasteners.

Later

Bubble level, with the height setting

Stick the bar level on the outer edge of Panel A's driver-side rail at mid-span when the panels are leveled.
